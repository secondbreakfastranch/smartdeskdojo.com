05 // THE KEYBOARD TRAY PROBLEM NOBODY TALKS ABOUT

The #1 complaint in Amazon converter reviews: keyboard tray depth. The VIVO 32" ships with 10.25 inches. The FlexiSpot M2B has 10.6. That's barely enough for a keyboard with no wrist rest , add a gel pad and your palms hang off the edge.

Quick Check: Measure your keyboard depth including any wrist rest. Add 2 inches for comfortable hand positioning. If the total exceeds the converter's tray depth, you'll be uncomfortable within 20 minutes of typing. The Ergotron WorkFit-T (12.5") and FlexiSpot M7B (11.8") are the only converters in this roundup with genuinely usable keyboard tray depth for full-size keyboards plus wrist support.

06 // CLAMP VS. FREESTANDING: WHICH MOUNTING STYLE WINS

Freestanding converters (VariDesk, FlexiSpot) sit on your desk , zero installation. But they eat up surface even when collapsed. Clamp-mounted converters (Ergotron WorkFit-T, some VIVO models) bolt to the desk edge, freeing surface underneath, but require 1 inch of overhang and can't move between desks.

If your desk is under 48 inches wide, go clamp-mounted. You can't afford to lose a third of your surface to the converter's base. If your desk is 60+ inches and you've got the real estate, freestanding gives you the flexibility to slide the converter off entirely when you want a clean desk for non-computer tasks.
