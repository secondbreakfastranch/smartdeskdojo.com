01 // Split vs Wave: What Actually Matters

Ergonomic keyboards fall into two camps. Split keyboards physically separate into two halves you position shoulder-width apart. This eliminates ulnar deviation and lets you tent each half at an angle. The tradeoff: a 1-2 week learning curve. Wave keyboards keep a single-piece body but curve the key layout into a gentle arc. They feel natural within a day and don't require repositioning muscle memory.

Split vs Wave Decision: If you have diagnosed RSI or type 6+ hours daily, get a split keyboard. The shoulder-width positioning and tenting angle produce measurably lower muscle activation. If you're switching preventively or share a desk, a wave keyboard gives 80% of the benefit with near-zero adjustment time.
